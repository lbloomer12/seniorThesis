# Senior Thesis Repo: Shot Tracker

## Software Requirements Specification for the ShotTracker app

## Introduction

### Purpose

The purpose of this document is to outline the functional and non-functional requirements of the ShotTracker app. This app is designed to help lacrosse players and coaches get valuable advanced analytics by tracking shots faced during games, including where each shot came from on the field, where it went in the goal, and whether it resulted in a save or a goal. This specification serves as a guideline as to what features the app will have, and the use cases it will fulfill by the time of completion.

The key goals of the app are:

- To enable easy and seamless data entry of lacrosse shots, including shot location, goal placement, and outcome.
- To produce advanced analytics based off the data entered, broken down per player and per game.
- To visualize shot data through field and goal diagrams so patterns in shot placement and save performance are easy to see.
